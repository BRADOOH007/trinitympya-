### 2. Submit Sitemap to Google (10 minutes)

2. Click "Add Property" → Enter: `https://www.trinitybusexpress.com`

3. Verify ownership (choose one method):

   - DNS record
   - Google Analytics
   - Google Tag Manager

4. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in left menu

5. Enter: `sitemap.xml`

6. Click "Submit"

7. Click "Request Indexing" for these priority pages:
   - Homepage: `/`

   - Top blog: `/blog/nairobi-to-kampala-bus-travel-guide`

### 3. Set Up 301 Redirects (CRITICAL - 5 minutes)

**In Vercel Dashboard:**

1. Go to your project settings

2. Navigate to "Domains"

3. Add redirect rule:
   ```
   Source: trinityexpressbusonlinebooking.com/*
   Destination: trinitybusexpress.com/$1
   Permanent: Yes (301)
   ```

4. This preserves your old SEO rankings!

**Why this matters:**
- Google has indexed your old domain
- Without redirects, you lose all that SEO value
- 301 redirects transfer 90-99% of link equity
